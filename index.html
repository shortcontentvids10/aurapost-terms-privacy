Terms of Service

Last updated September 2026

What AuraPost is

AuraPost is not a public product, service, or platform. It is a private automation pipeline, built and run by one person, that discovers short sports clips, generates a branded "aura ranking" edit, and publishes the result to that same person's own YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok accounts.

Who can use it

AuraPost is not offered to the public and has no external users, sign-up flow, or accounts. It authenticates only against social media accounts that its operator personally owns and controls, using each platform's own developer API and OAuth consent flow.

No warranty

AuraPost is provided as-is, for its operator's personal use only, with no warranty of any kind and no guarantee of uptime, accuracy, or fitness for any particular purpose.

Changes

This page may be updated as the tool's scope changes. Continued personal use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Data collected

AuraPost does not collect data from, or about, any third party. It has no users other than its operator. The only accounts it interacts with are social media accounts the operator personally owns.

What is stored, and where

  • Source video clips and rendered output videos, stored locally on the operator's own machine.
  • OAuth credentials (access and refresh tokens) for the operator's own YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok accounts, stored locally and never transmitted to any third party beyond the platform that issued them.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers, or third-party data-sharing of any kind.

Platform permissions

Where AuraPost requests API scopes from YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok (for example, permission to upload video on the operator's behalf), those permissions are used solely to publish the operator's own content to the operator's own account, and for no other purpose.
